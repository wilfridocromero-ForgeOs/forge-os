# ORVESEN Color System

Version: 1.0

---

# Philosophy

Colors should communicate hierarchy.

Never decoration.

Every color must have a specific purpose.

The interface should feel calm, premium and professional.

ORVESEN avoids unnecessary colors and visual noise.

---

# Rules

## Backgrounds

Dark backgrounds create focus.

Avoid bright backgrounds.

---

## Text

Always maximize readability.

Never reduce contrast for aesthetic reasons.

---

## Gold

Gold is reserved.

If everything is gold,

nothing feels important.

---

## Contrast

Interfaces should be comfortable during long working sessions.

Avoid pure black against pure white when possible.

Prefer soft contrast.

---

## Future

Light Mode may exist.

Dark Mode is the primary ORVESEN experience.
